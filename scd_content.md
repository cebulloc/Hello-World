# Overview

This system is designed for:

- Specific Carbohydrate Diet (SCD) compliant
- Minimal waste through ingredient overlap
- Batch cooking 2-3 times per week maximum
- Family-friendly (adults and kids together, no separate cooking)
- Freezer scalability up to 30 days

Core proteins: Chicken, Beef, Pork
Core method: Roast + slow cook + bone-broth base system

Reference for legal/illegal foods: scdrecipe.com/legal-illegal-list. All ingredients below were chosen to fall on the SCD legal list (no grains, no sugar, no starches, no processed foods, no non-compliant sauces).

# Master Shopping List (Low Waste)

## Proteins
- Chicken thighs or breasts (6-8 lb)
- Beef chuck roast (3-4 lb)
- Pork loin (2-3 lb)

## Vegetables
- Zucchini (6-8)

## Fats and Basics

- Lemons (4-6)
- Sea salt
- Black pepper (optional, depending on strictness)
- Apple cider vinegar (SCD-legal if tolerated)

## Optional SCD Fruit
- Apples
- Blueberries
- Bananas (ripe, limited)

# Core Cook Session (2-3 Hours)

## 1. Chicken (base protein)
- Olive oil + salt + garlic
- Bake at 400 deg F for 35-45 min
- Shred half, slice/leave half whole

## 2. Beef (slow cook, set-and-forget)
- Chuck roast + onion + garlic + salt
- Slow cooker or Dutch oven, 6-8 hours
- Shred completely when done

## 3. Pork Loin (roast)
- Olive oil + rosemary + salt
- Bake at 375 deg F for 45-60 min
- Slice thin for reuse meals

## 4. Vegetables (roasted tray)
- Zucchini + carrots + broccoli
- Olive oil + salt
- Bake at 400 deg F for about 25 min

## 5. Bone Broth (zero-waste win)
- Chicken bones + beef scraps + onion + celery
- Simmer 12-24 hours

## 6. Cauliflower Rice (rice substitute)
- Pulse cauliflower in a food processor
- Saute in olive oil + salt

- Protein: 2 palm-sized servings per day

# SCD-Compliant Sauce System (Key to Success)

This is the most important section. Rotating sauces is what keeps the same ingredients from becoming boring. All are SCD legal: no starch, no sugar, no dairy (unless you make 24-hour SCD yogurt separately).

## 1. Garlic Lemon Olive Oil Drizzle (base sauce)
- Olive oil + fresh lemon juice + minced or roasted garlic + salt
- Bright, Mediterranean feel. Use on everything. Prevents "bland chicken fatigue."

## 2. Bone Broth Reduction Glaze (gravy replacement)
- Simmer broth until slightly thickened, add salt + rosemary
- Pour over beef or pork

## 3. Roasted Carrot Puree Sauce (kid favorite)
- Roasted carrots + olive oil + splash of broth, blended smooth
- Naturally sweet; great for kids, pork, and chicken

## 4. Zucchini Herb Sauce (green / pesto substitute)
- Cooked zucchini + olive oil + garlic + basil or parsley, blended lightly
- Works like pesto without dairy

## 5. Onion Jam (SCD version)
- Slow-cooked onions (very slow, no sugar) + olive oil + salt
- Adds sweetness without violating SCD

## 6. Apple Cinnamon Meat Glaze (kids love this)
- Cooked apple + cinnamon + broth reduction
- Great with pork or chicken

## 7. Comfort Soup Base (reset meal)
- Bone broth + shredded chicken or beef + carrots + spinach
- Your reset meal when everything feels repetitive

# Freezer System (30-Day Scaling)

You already have the core week. To scale, you do not add recipes - you increase batch volume.

## Weekly rotation
- Week A: Chicken heavy, beef moderate, pork light
- Week B: Pork heavy, chicken moderate, beef light
- Week C: Beef heavy, chicken moderate, pork light
- Week D: Clean out freezer + mixed meals

## Batch storage targets (about 30 days)
- 20-24 chicken portions
- 16-20 beef portions
- 14-18 pork portions
- 20+ vegetable packs
- 15-20 broth packs

Store everything FoodSaver / vacuum-sealed and flat-packed for fast freezing and reheating.

# Kid System (Ages 9 and 6, No Separate Cooking)

Most strict diets fail on acceptance, not rules. Solve it with presentation, not different food.

Rule: same food, different format.
- Adults: plated meals
- Kids: dip + bowl + finger-food system

## Kid-approved formats
- Dip meals: chicken strips + carrot puree sauce + zucchini sauce
- Soup bowls: broth + shredded meat + soft veg
- Meat + sweet veg combo: pork + roasted carrots (natural sweetness helps acceptance)
- Snack boxes: sliced chicken + SCD fruit + soft carrots

## Fruit strategy
Use apples, blueberries, and bananas (if tolerated) as a side reward, a sauce base, or a snack bridge - not as the main meal.

# Strict SCD Reminders

Avoid completely:
- Grains, rice, potatoes, and other starches
- Sugar, maple syrup, honey (unless your strict SCD stage allows honey)
- Processed meats
- Soy and dairy (unless homemade 24-hour SCD yogurt)
- Store-bought sauces and dressings

---

# How This System Stays Non-Boring

You do NOT change ingredients. You rotate four things:

1. Sauce rotation - same chicken becomes 5+ different flavors
2. Texture rotation - shredded, sliced, stew, pan-seared reheats
3. Temperature rotation - hot bowl, soup, warm plate
4. Pairings - mix which protein meets which veg and sauce

Same food, different experience. This matters more than adding new recipes.
