# Overview

This system is designed for:

- Specific Carbohydrate Diet (SCD) compliant
- Minimal waste through ingredient overlap
- ONE weekly prep session - cook once, eat all week (no mid-week cooking)
- Family-friendly (adults and kids together, no separate cooking)
- Freezer scalability up to 30 days

Core proteins: Chicken, Beef, Pork
Core method: One big roast + slow cook + bone-broth session, then grab-and-reheat all week.

Reference for legal/illegal foods: scdrecipe.com/legal-illegal-list. All ingredients below were chosen to fall on the SCD legal list (no grains, no sugar, no starches, no processed foods, no non-compliant sauces).

IMPORTANT NOTE ON OATS: Real oats (and all grains) are NOT SCD legal - they are on the illegal list. This plan uses an SCD-legal "n'oatmeal" (a nut-based hot or cold porridge) that gives you the same warm-bowl, oatmeal-style breakfast while staying compliant. Recipe is in the Breakfast section.

# One Weekly Prep Session (Cook Once - About 3 Hours)

The whole point: run the oven, slow cooker, and stovetop at the same time so you finish everything in a single session and do not cook again all week. Suggested order so nothing waits on anything else:

## Step 0 - Start the slow cooker FIRST (it runs 6-8 hrs in the background)
- Beef chuck roast + onion + garlic + salt -> slow cooker on LOW
- This finishes while you do everything else; shred at the end.

## Step 1 - Oven block (proteins + veg roast together)
- Chicken: olive oil + salt + garlic, 400 deg F, 35-45 min. Shred half, slice/leave half.
- Pork loin: olive oil + rosemary + salt, 375 deg F, 45-60 min. Slice thin.
- Vegetable tray: zucchini + carrots + broccoli, olive oil + salt, 400 deg F, about 25 min.
- (Stagger trays; the oven can hold all of these across the session.)

## Step 2 - Stovetop block (while oven runs)
- Cauliflower rice: pulse cauliflower, saute in olive oil + salt.
- Sauteed onions (big batch) - base flavor + onion jam sauce.
- Hard-boil 8-12 eggs for grab-and-go breakfasts/snacks.

## Step 3 - Bone broth (zero waste, set and forget)
- Chicken bones + beef scraps + onion + celery -> simmer 12-24 hrs (overnight is fine).

## Step 4 - Breakfast prep (do once, eat all week)
- Egg muffins (recipe below): bake a dozen, refrigerate/freeze.
- N'oatmeal dry mix: pre-mix the nut base in a jar so mornings are 3 minutes.

## Step 5 - Cool, portion, store
- Portion proteins + veg into containers (or vacuum-seal flat for freezer).
- Shred the beef from the slow cooker once it is done.

Result: one session, then it is all reheat-and-eat. No 2-3x per week cooking.

# Breakfast Options (Hot, Cold, Eggs, and More)

## A) Hot N'oatmeal (SCD "oatmeal" swap - warm bowl)
Makes 1 serving; scale up and pre-mix the dry base for the week.
- 2 tbsp almond flour
- 2 tbsp chopped pecans or walnuts
- 1 tbsp unsweetened shredded coconut
- 1/2 ripe banana, mashed (natural sweetness + binds it)
- 1 egg (optional, for a thick "porridge" body) OR 1 tbsp nut butter
- Cinnamon to taste
- Splash of water or SCD yogurt to loosen

Method: stir everything in a small pot over low heat (or microwave 60-90 sec), stirring until warm and thick like oatmeal. Top with blueberries, more cinnamon, or a tiny drizzle of honey if tolerated.

## B) Cold N'oatmeal (overnight style)
- Same dry base (almond flour + nuts + coconut + cinnamon)
- Stir into 1/2 cup homemade 24-hour SCD yogurt
- Add mashed or sliced banana + berries
- Refrigerate overnight; eat cold. Great for hot mornings or grab-and-go.

## C) Egg Breakfasts (the workhorse - all SCD legal)
- Scrambled eggs in butter/ghee with sauteed spinach + onions
- Veggie omelet: eggs + zucchini + bell pepper + onion
- Egg + shredded chicken or beef scramble (uses your prepped protein)
- Soft/hard-boiled eggs from the prep batch (grab-and-go)
- Fried eggs over cauliflower rice with olive oil + salt

## D) Make-Ahead Egg Muffins (prep once, reheat all week)
- Whisk 10-12 eggs + salt
- Stir in chopped spinach, onion, bell pepper, and/or leftover shredded meat
- Pour into a greased muffin tin
- Bake 350 deg F for about 20-22 min
- Refrigerate 4-5 days or freeze. Reheat 30-45 sec.

## E) Other Yummy SCD Breakfast Add-ons
- Fruit + nut bowl: banana, blueberries, strawberries, chopped pecans
- SCD yogurt + berries + cinnamon + a few nuts
- Apple "saute": sliced apple cooked in butter + cinnamon (pairs with eggs)
- Almond-flour pancakes (egg + almond flour + mashed banana, pan-fried) - weekend treat

# SCD-Compliant Sauce System (Key to Success)

Rotating sauces is what keeps the same ingredients from getting boring. All SCD legal: no starch, no sugar, no dairy (unless homemade 24-hour SCD yogurt).

## 1. Garlic Lemon Olive Oil Drizzle (base sauce)
- Olive oil + fresh lemon juice + minced or roasted garlic + salt. Use on everything.

## 2. Bone Broth Reduction Glaze (gravy replacement)
- Simmer broth until slightly thickened, add salt + rosemary. Over beef or pork.

## 3. Roasted Carrot Puree Sauce (kid favorite)
- Roasted carrots + olive oil + splash of broth, blended smooth. Naturally sweet.

## 4. Zucchini Herb Sauce (green / pesto substitute)
- Cooked zucchini + olive oil + garlic + basil or parsley, blended lightly.

## 5. Onion Jam (SCD version)
- Slow-cooked onions (very slow, no sugar) + olive oil + salt. Adds sweetness.

## 6. Apple Cinnamon Meat Glaze (kids love this)
- Cooked apple + cinnamon + broth reduction. Great with pork or chicken.

## 7. Comfort Soup Base (reset meal)
- Bone broth + shredded chicken or beef + carrots + spinach.

# Freezer System (Scale to 30 Days - Still One Session)

You do not add cook sessions - you increase batch volume in the SAME weekly cook and freeze the extra.

## Weekly rotation
- Week A: Chicken heavy, beef moderate, pork light
- Week B: Pork heavy, chicken moderate, beef light
- Week C: Beef heavy, chicken moderate, pork light
- Week D: Clean out freezer + mixed meals

## Batch storage targets (about 30 days)
- 20-24 chicken portions
- 16-20 beef portions
- 14-18 pork portions
- 20+ vegetable packs
- 15-20 broth packs
- 1-2 dozen egg muffins (freeze well)

Vacuum-seal / FoodSaver flat-pack everything for fast freezing and reheating.

# Kid System (Ages 9 and 6, No Separate Cooking)

Same food, different format.
- Adults: plated meals
- Kids: dip + bowl + finger-food system

## Kid-approved formats
- Dip meals: chicken strips + carrot puree sauce + zucchini sauce
- Soup bowls: broth + shredded meat + soft veg
- Meat + sweet veg: pork + roasted carrots (natural sweetness helps acceptance)
- Snack boxes: sliced chicken + SCD fruit + soft carrots
- Breakfast win: egg muffins and hot n'oatmeal are usually kid favorites

## Fruit strategy
Use apples, blueberries, strawberries, and bananas (if tolerated) as a side reward, a sauce base, or a snack bridge - not as the main meal.

# Strict SCD Reminders

Avoid completely:
- Grains, rice, potatoes, OATS, corn, and other starches
- Sugar, maple syrup (honey is the only legal sweetener, used sparingly)
- Processed foods and processed meats
- Soy and most dairy (unless homemade 24-hour SCD yogurt)
- Store-bought sauces and dressings

# How This System Stays Non-Boring

You do NOT change ingredients. You rotate four things:

1. Sauce rotation - same chicken becomes 5+ different flavors
2. Texture rotation - shredded, sliced, stew, pan-seared reheats
3. Temperature rotation - hot bowl, soup, warm plate, cold n'oatmeal
4. Pairings - mix which protein meets which veg and sauce

Same food, different experience.
